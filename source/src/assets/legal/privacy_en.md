# Jichen Calendar Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 11, 2026**

Welcome to **Jichen Calendar** (“the App”). This policy explains how we handle your information.

## 1. Overview

Jichen Calendar is a **local-first** lunar calendar and life assistant. **The current version works without an account.** Your reminders, anniversaries, favorite dates, and city preferences are stored **on your device** by default.

The free version may show ads served by Google AdMob. If an ad fails to load, the ad slot is hidden and core features remain available.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Information you provide

- **Reminders & anniversaries**: titles, dates, notes, relationship/type metadata (stored locally).
- **Favorite dates** (local).
- **Weather city**: manually chosen or suggested after location permission (stored locally; location is used only to suggest a city, not tracked as a history).
- **Backup password**: used only on-device for AES encryption; we do not collect or store it.

### Automatically collected

- **Weather requests** for your selected city via public APIs such as Open-Meteo (no personal identity).
- **Ad requests**: the free version requests and displays app open and banner ads through Google AdMob. The ads SDK may use device identifiers, app information, ad interactions, coarse location, and similar data to serve ads, cap frequency, measure performance, and prevent fraud, subject to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and AdMob policies. We may show privacy choices through Google UMP; if you decline personalized ads, non-personalized ads may still appear.
- **Diagnostics**: third-party crash analytics are not enabled by default in the current version.

### We do not collect by default

- Contacts
- Photo library contents (unless you choose via system share/save)
- Payment or government ID data
- Full reminder/anniversary content (**not uploaded to our servers in the current version**)

## 3. How We Use Information

- Calendar, almanac, date picking, reminders, blessings, and sharing
- Weather for your chosen city when authorized
- Ads after you accept applicable privacy choices
- Product improvement and bug fixes

## 4. Storage & Security

- **Local**: SharedPreferences and similar on-device storage.
- **Local backup**: exports use **AES-256-GCM**; keep your password and files safe.
- **Retention**: uninstalling removes local data.

## 5. Third-Party Services

- System share / file picker / photo library
- Open-Meteo (weather summaries)
- Google AdMob (app open ads, banner ads, ad measurement, fraud prevention)

We do not sell personal information.

## 6. Your Rights & Deletion

- Edit or delete reminders, anniversaries, and favorites in-app
- Export/import/undo import under Data & Backup
- Manage location, photo library, tracking, and notification permissions in iOS Settings
- **Uninstall** to remove all local data

## 7. Traditional Culture Disclaimer

Lunar, solar term, almanac, and festival poem content is for **cultural reference only**, not professional advice.

## 8. Children

The App is intended primarily for adults and is **not directed at children under 13**. Minors should use it with guardian consent.

## 9. Updates

We may update this policy; the in-app Privacy Policy page will show the latest version.
